Recently I described what I “do" to some students. It was my first experience of presenting to such a large audience via Zoom (something we've all got used to by now). It was a bit clunky, I made mistakes, and it felt awkward. But I stuck with it and I'm making it public.

**TLDR**

There is a depth to the questions we must ask ourselves and others. This process of developing those questions is a craft in itself.

It’s the dance between those who are directly and loosely connected to the questions we pose. This is where a brighter perspective burns.

It's about building a concious system that gets the best out of yourself and others, because this is the only way we should be working.

It's realising how to make art from what you capture and discover. This is what moves people and brings us meaning.
